PayarMove – Second Brief
Rental Schedule Management, Stellar Payment Reconciliation & Landlord–Tenant Dashboard Improvements
Project Duration: 30 Days
Blockchain: Stellar Testnet
Project Type: Existing MVP Enhancement
Focus: Rental Obligations, Payment Verification, Reconciliation, Dashboard Improvements & User Validation
1. Project Overview
PayarMove is a Stellar-powered rental payment platform designed to help property managers, landlords, and tenants manage rental obligations and payments through a transparent digital system.
The first development phase established the foundation for rental payment workflows and Stellar integration.
The second tranche focuses on expanding PayarMove beyond individual payment transactions into a more complete rental payment management experience.
Property managers will be able to assign tenants to properties, create rental schedules, define payment amounts and due dates, and monitor recurring rent obligations.
Tenants will be able to view upcoming rent, make payments, track outstanding balances, and download receipts.
The platform will also integrate Stellar transaction verification and reconciliation to match confirmed payments to the correct rental obligations, support partial payments, prevent duplicate credits, and update outstanding balances automatically.
Primary objective: Deliver an enhanced PayarMove MVP that supports recurring rental obligations, reliable Stellar payment reconciliation, accurate outstanding balances, and functional landlord and tenant dashboards, validated with at least 10 users.

B. Recurring rent obligations
The system should automatically generate rental obligations based on the configured schedule.
For example, a tenant assigned to a property with monthly rent of 300 USDC would have a separate payment obligation generated for each rental period.
Each obligation should have its own unique reference, due date, amount due, amount paid, outstanding balance, and payment status.

Payment reconciliation rules
Confirm the payment exists on Stellar Testnet.
Verify that the transaction succeeded.
Confirm the recipient matches the landlord's registered wallet.
Validate the payment asset.
Match the payment to a valid tenant and rental obligation.
Verify the payment amount.
Credit only confirmed payments.
Allow multiple valid partial payments toward the same obligation.
Prevent the same payment operation from being credited more than once.
Flag unmatched or excess payments for review.
Recalculate the outstanding balance after every verified credit.
Preserve reconciliation records when the monitoring service restarts.
Implementation consideration: The system should use a unique obligation reference or another reliable matching mechanism. It must not assume that the wallet sending funds always belongs to the tenant, since a third party may pay on a tenant's behalf.
For transactions containing multiple payment operations, reconciliation should identify individual payment operations rather than treating the transaction hash alone as a unique payment.
Reconciliation formula

OutstandingRent=(0,AmountDue-VerifiedPayments)
An obligation is fully paid when verified credited payments equal its amount due. Excess funds should be flagged rather than silently applied to unrelated rental periods.
Why this matters: Reduces manual rent reconciliation, prevents duplicate credits, and ensures landlords and tenants see accurate payment balances.
Acceptance criteria: The system correctly verifies and matches Stellar Testnet rental payments, reconciles partial and full payments, prevents duplicate credits, and updates outstanding balances automatically.

11. Definition of Done
The PayarMove second tranche will be considered complete when landlords and property managers can create rental schedules, assign tenants, generate recurring rent obligations, and monitor rental collections.
Tenants must be able to view their rent schedules, make full or partial Stellar Testnet payments, track outstanding balances, and download verified payment receipts.
The system must accurately verify rental payments, reconcile them to the correct obligations, prevent duplicate credits, and automatically update balances and overdue statuses.
At least 10 users must complete structured testing, with feedback, transaction evidence, and critical fixes documented.
Development priority: Rental schedules and tenant assignments → recurring payment obligations → Stellar payment verification and reconciliation → enhanced dashboards and receipts → user validation. The focus is to transform PayarMove from a basic rental payment experience into a practical rental obligation management platform.
